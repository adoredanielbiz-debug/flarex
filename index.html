FAQ

Sign up with your email address, set a password and confirm the verification link we send you. The whole thing takes a couple of minutes — you only need to complete identity verification before your first deposit or trade.

KYC (Know Your Customer) is the identity check every regulated financial provider has to run. As an FSCA-licensed ODP we verify your ID document and a proof of address, which keeps the platform compliant and keeps bad actors out of everyone's order book.

Fund your account by local bank transfer or instant EFT through our payment partners. ZAR deposits usually clear within minutes during banking hours; other currencies settle on the next business day.

Once your account is funded, pick a market, enter the amount you want in either crypto or your local currency, and choose a market or limit order. You'll see the fee and the exact amount you receive before you confirm.

Fees are charged as a percentage of each trade and scale down with your 30-day volume. Makers who add liquidity pay less than takers who remove it, and the applicable rate is always shown on the order ticket before you confirm.

Withdraw crypto to any whitelisted wallet address, or send fiat back to a bank account in your own name. Crypto withdrawals are broadcast once security checks clear; fiat withdrawals follow standard banking settlement times.

Turn on two-factor authentication, whitelist your withdrawal addresses, and use a password you don't reuse anywhere else. We hold customer assets with institutional custody and will never ask you for your password or 2FA codes.
